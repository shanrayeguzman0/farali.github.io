How to Use Anime Rank List?

Anime Rank List is a personal anime tracking and ranking tool designed to help you organize, rate, and analyze your anime watching experience. Here's a quick guide on how to use it:

1. Adding Anime to Your Ranking List

To add an anime to your ranking list, click the "Add Anime" button in the header. Fill in the anime's name, total episodes, release year, and optionally upload a cover image and summary, and you can add your favorite character. Once added, you can rate the anime and your favorite character based on various criteria.

2. Rating Anime

Click on any anime in your ranking list to open the rating modal. Here you can rate different aspects of the anime such as storyline, dialogues, art style, and more. The overall score will be calculated based on your ratings, you can view your rated episodes in the modal graph.

3. Managing Your Currently Watching List

Switch to the "Currently Watching" tab to manage the anime you're currently watching. You can add new anime to this list, update your progress, and rate episodes as you watch them.

4. Profile System

The "Profile" tab shows your personal anime journey stats, including your Rank, Level, XP progress, total episodes watched, achievements, and favorite anime. Complete more anime to level up and climb the ranks!

5. Memories

In the "Memories" tab, you can create a personalized collection of your anime journey. Upload photos, add titles and descriptions to capture your memories and experiences with each anime.

6. Exporting and Importing Data

You can export your entire ranking list and currently watching list as a JSON file for backup or sharing purposes. To import data, click the "Import" button and select a previously exported JSON file.

7. Deleting Data

If you want to start fresh or remove all your data, click the "Delete All" button. Please note that this action is irreversible.

If you have any questions or need further assistance, feel free to reach out!

Definition and Guides of Rating System

Storyline (20 pts)

Evaluate the plot's originality, coherence, and engagement. Consider how well the story is structured, the pacing, plot twists, and whether it kept you interested throughout. A strong storyline should have clear progression, meaningful conflicts, and satisfying resolution.

Clarity (20 pts)

Rate how clear and understandable the anime was. This includes the coherence of the plot, the clarity of character motivations, logical consistency, and how well the anime conveyed its themes and messages without confusion.

Art Style (10 pts)

Evaluate the visual aesthetics of the anime, including character design, background art, color palette, and overall visual presentation. Consider how well the art style suited the anime's tone and genre.

Animation (10 pts)

Assess the quality of animation and motion, including the fluidity of movement, use of special effects, action sequences, and how well the animation enhanced the storytelling and visual impact.

Experience (20 pts)

Assess the overall emotional impact and immersion of the anime. This includes how emotionally resonant the story was, the intensity of key moments, your personal enjoyment, and how well the anime made you feel connected to its world and characters.

Music Opening (OP) (10 pts)

Evaluate the opening theme music, including its composition, how well it fit the anime's mood and tone, visual presentation of the opening sequence, and its overall appeal and memorability.

Ending (ED) (10 pts)

Evaluate the ending theme music, including its composition, how well it fit the anime's mood and provided closure, visual presentation of the ending sequence, and its overall appeal and emotional resonance.

What is Best Features?

Anime Rank List offers a range of features to enhance your anime tracking and ranking experience:

Auto-save

Your data is automatically saved, ensuring that your rankings and watch list are preserved even if you close the tab or refresh the page.

Export/Import

Easily export your ranking list and currently watching list as a JSON file for backup or sharing. You can also import data from a previously exported file to restore your lists.
