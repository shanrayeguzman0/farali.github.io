How to Use Anime Rank List?

Anime Rank List is a personal anime tracking and ranking tool designed to help you organize, rate, and analyze your anime watching experience. Here's a quick guide on how to use it:

1. Adding Anime to Your Ranking List

To add an anime to your ranking list, click the "Add Anime" button in the header. Fill in the anime's name, total episodes, release year, and optionally upload a cover image and summary, and add your favorite character. Once added, you can rate the anime and your favorite character based on various criteria.

2. Rating Anime

Click on any anime in your ranking list to open the rating modal. Here you can rate different aspects of the anime such as storyline, dialogues, art style, and more. The overall score will be calculated based on your ratings.

3. Managing Your Currently Watching List

Switch to the "Currently Watching" tab to manage the anime you're currently watching. You can add new anime to this list, update your progress, and rate episodes as you watch them.

4. Viewing Statistics

The "Statistics" tab provides insights into your anime watching habits, including average scores, genre distribution, and more. Use this data to discover trends in your preferences.

5. Exporting and Importing Data

You can export your entire ranking list and currently watching list as a JSON file for backup or sharing purposes. To import data, click the "Import" button and select a previously exported JSON file.

6. Deleting Data

If you want to start fresh or remove all your data, click the "Delete All" button. Please note that this action is irreversible.

If you have any questions or need further assistance, feel free to reach out!

Definition and Guides of Rating System

Storyline

Evaluate the plot's originality and engagement. Consider how well the story is structured, the pacing, and whether it kept you interested throughout.

Dialogues

Assess the quality of the dialogues, including how natural they felt and how well they contributed to character development and plot progression.

Clarity

Rate how clear and understandable the anime was. This includes the coherence of the plot, the clarity of character motivations, and how well the anime conveyed its themes.

Art Style

Evaluate the visual aesthetics of the anime, including character design, background art, and overall animation quality. Consider how well the art style suited the anime's tone and genre.

Motion / FX

Assess the quality of the animation, including the fluidity of motion, the use of special effects, and how well the animation enhanced the storytelling.

Music Opening

Evaluate the opening theme music, including its composition, how well it fit the anime's mood, and its overall appeal.

ED

Evaluate the ending theme music, including its composition, how well it fit the anime's mood, and its overall appeal.

Concept

Assess the originality and creativity of the anime's concept, including its setting, themes, and overall premise and accuracy from the title anime.

SFX

Evaluate the quality of sound effects used in the anime, including how well they enhanced the atmosphere and contributed to the overall experience.

Attractive

Assess the overall appeal of the anime, including how engaging and captivating it was from start to finish, sometimes anime can attract you wihtout knowing that you are already enjoyed in the show.

Voice Action

Evaluate the quality of voice acting in the anime, including how well the voice actors portrayed their characters and conveyed emotions.

Experience(EXP) intensity

Assess the intensity of the overall experience, including how emotionally impactful and immersive the anime was.

Experience(EXP) Enjoyment

Evaluate the overall enjoyment of the anime, including how much you personally liked it and how satisfying the experience was.

Experience(EXP) Expressive or Emotional Expression

Assess the anime's ability to express emotions and convey feelings, including how well it made you feel and connected with the characters and story.

Character(CHAR) Personalities

Evaluate the depth and complexity of the characters' personalities, including how well they were developed and how relatable they were, you can tell if you like their personalities how they act.

Character(CHAR) Styles

Assess the uniqueness and appeal of the characters' styles, including their fashion, design, and overall visual presentation.

What is Best Features?

Anime Rank List offers a range of features to enhance your anime tracking and ranking experience:
